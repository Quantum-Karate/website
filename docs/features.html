Check your work

A results log you can read honestly

Each practice cycle is saved to a log on your computer. Open Practice results to see, for one strategy and one lane: number of trades, win rate, average win and average loss, worst drawdown, an equity curve, and time in practice.

Under 30 closed trades, the page says “Too few trades to judge yet” and does not offer a verdict. It always carries this line: “Simulated Practice results. Not a prediction of future or Live performance.”

Already have backtest numbers? Import a CSV or JSON file and see them side by side with your practice results. You can also export the log to CSV. Import and export stay on your computer.

03An option, not the point

Live is optional, and it is your call.

Most people can stay in Practice as long as they like. If you decide to go further, Live is opt-in and every step is yours:

  1. You add your own broker keys. No keys ship in the download.
  2. You turn Live on yourself. In Safety, choose “Turn on Live trading”, tick three boxes, and type LIVE to confirm. You can return to Practice at any time.
  3. Approving orders. When Live orders are set up through the optional Neural Agency Engine order service, each order is held until you approve it in the “Live order approvals” panel in the console on your computer. An order you do not approve in time expires and is not sent. That order service is off by default.
  4. Orders are capped. The default cap is $15 per order (options premium, crypto order size, and Seduma’s own Tradier connection). You can change it in your settings, and a bad setting falls back to the small default.
  5. Money out stays off. Withdrawals, transfers between accounts, and payouts are refused.

Emergency stop refuses new orders. It does not close open positions or cancel orders already at your broker.
