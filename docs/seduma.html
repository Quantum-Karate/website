01How it works

Four plain steps. Practice first.

Seduma is strategy-free. You bring the rules. Seduma runs them in Practice by default, and nothing goes Live unless you say so.

  1. Step 01

    Bring your rules

    A strategy is the set of rules that decides when to place an order. You bring your own. Seduma does not pick, recommend, or generate trades.

  2. Step 02

    Start in Practice

    Practice is the default start. Fills are simulated and no real money moves. Your first Practice run needs no Live or real broker keys.

  3. Step 03

    Know how to stop

    Emergency stop makes Seduma refuse to send new orders until you release it. It does not close open positions or cancel orders already at your broker.

    Seduma keeps an audit trail: a record written before it contacts any outside service.

  4. Step 04

    Live, only if you choose

    Live is optional and off by default. Finishing setup does not turn it on. To elect Live, you confirm it yourself by typing LIVE. It does not switch on by itself.

    Emergency stop stays available in Practice and in Live. It does not close open positions.

First run

Setup stays Paper.

First-run setup has four steps: Welcome to Paper Practice, Safety check, Try a practice run, and You’re set — setup stays Paper.

The Safety check records that you read the Paper disclosures. It does not turn on Live. Completing setup does not turn on Live — you choose later.

Live election

Live is a choice, not a default.

If you ever elect Live, Seduma won’t turn it on until every item is checked and LIVE is typed. Cancelling keeps you in Practice.

Connecting a broker does not unlock Live. Emergency stop stays available in Practice and in Live. It does not close open positions.

02Honest Practice

Three plain lines.

  1. 01

  2. 02

    Paper/Practice P&L is not a promise of live results.

  3. 03

P&L is the running gain or loss on trades. A broker’s paper account also differs from that broker’s live market, and Seduma does not paper over that.
